Deal Math — Privacy Policy

日本語

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Summary
The app has no sign-up and does not collect your name or email address.
Prices and quantities you enter are only used for the calculation on your device. They are not saved or sent anywhere.
The free version shows ads from Google (AdMob). To show ads, device information is sent to Google.
You decide whether ads may be personalized, using the iPhone's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

This policy applies to the app published as “どれ得” in Japan and under local names in other countries (for example “Deal Math”).

1. Information the app does not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, address, contacts, photos or precise location. Prices, quantities and discounts you enter are not saved or sent. The app contains no analytics tools.

2. Information stored on your device

Only the following is stored on your iPhone. It is never sent to us, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

  • Display settings (theme colour, light or dark mode, key sounds, etc.)
  • Counts and dates used to decide when to show the review request and the tracking prompt (number of comparisons, first day of use, last request)

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version uses AdMob, an advertising service of Google LLC. To show ads, AdMob may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking (see 4)
  • Device type, OS version, language and approximate location (from your IP address)
  • Ad views and interactions

This information is handled under Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Personalized ads (App Tracking Transparency)

After you have used the app for a while, iOS asks you once whether you allow tracking.

  • If you allow it, the advertising identifier may be used to show ads tailored to you.
  • If you do not, the identifier is not used and you see non-personalized ads. The app works the same either way.

You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown first. You can change your choices later from “Ad privacy” in the app's settings.

5. Premium (in-app subscription)

Purchases, payments and cancellations of Premium are handled by Apple's App Store. We never receive your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether Premium is active. While Premium is active, no ads are shown.

6. Network use

Calculations run entirely on your iPhone and work offline. The app connects to the internet only to show ads and to buy or check Premium.

7. Camera, photos and location

Not used. The app never asks for these permissions.

8. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children of any age. Ads are shown in line with Google's policies.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above.
